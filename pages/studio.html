Grandfather

Looking out of the speeding car Lush greenery speeding by, The ecstatic smell of the wet ground, The air humming its tune on my glum face, There was a joy but overpowered with remorse The greenery reminding me of the past When he and I used to sit on the porch The stories, the memories, An erudite man yet so foolish The car now stopped. I stand on the porch, 25 years went by, The serenity replaced by the constant hum of the post office, The old house now a falling ruin, I ran in my mid-thirties, Today after years of him leaving, I stand still in his room short of breath, “Make your grandfather proud” he had said. Tears rolled down my cheeks, Sanity came back with the tug of my child’s hand. ©_the.big.blue_

The man less known

Leaving behind the town, Footsteps vanish into the night; They say he's a man less known, A man who steered clear of light. He walks alone with a stick, As if the stick knows all his ticks; The woods and him exchange secrets, And the hut lets him in without regrets. With days numbered and beard turned white, Eyes like they've seen too much of this shite, The wrinkles sustained and the knowing smile, Puts away all ambiguity, and the wobbly legs, Have walked a little more than a mile. Leaving behind the bustle, I talk to leaves that rustle; Reeking of whiskey, I frown, As I hear, I am the man less known. ©_the.big.blue_

Struggle

An imbecile smile at the end of the day A tired laugh and an empty core This is all he hopes for His daily was never pronounced, not for him He could fetch a cup of milk, rice enough to fill stomachs to the point of hungry. That was it He wondered when ostentatious cars passed by Maybe he'd go near one. Maybe his son will. Then fear sets in, fear of not providing, His son's impeccable smile makes him contemplate, His wife in a veil would come for a vain pleasure at the fall. Such is Life. Tired and asphyxiated, full of terror and diffidence. @_the.big.blue_

An Extricating Night

The starlit sky seems like a getaway, The sweet melodies playing in my head, Brings back memories long left behind. Some dark and some bright carries me away. It almost manages to bring a sly smile, But, not of joy, it's of days I endured, The night sky stretches for a mile. Reminding me of doubts that come with it... The sky grows overcast now, As if it's about to pour out its mind, But the wind keeps the water at bay. So, I think about how the cold wind, Keeps the heavy clouds and sweeps it away... The lights around me dim, As I submit to my whim, The knot had tightened a while, And so did my sly smile. ©_the.big.blue_

Finding Oblivion

By a lake in the twilight, I see you bright as the moonlight. I sit beside, to have an ounce of respite. “Hey!” you say looking my way. “Hi!” I reply trying to look away. Your captivating smile has me staring, My scared heart lights up to say “Let’s risk something daring” Unsure, I took a dive The warm water calmed my insides After long, my heart took off its binds You seem benign when we intertwine Amongst this dreary disquiet, I found my oblivion in the night. ©_the.big.blue_
