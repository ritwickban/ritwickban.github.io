Benchmarking Causal Discovery Algorithms for Scale Free Graphs

Simulation study report (PDF)

Simulation Python Causal Discovery

Hover to learn more

  • Benchmarked BOSS, FGES, PC, and DAGMA across Erdős–Rényi vs scale‑free DAGs (hub/sink structures).
  • Varied network size/degree and sample size; compared precision/recall/F1 and calibration metrics.
  • Key finding: BOSS was most robust on scale‑free topologies, maintaining high precision with stronger recall than alternatives.

Choose Better Hyperparameters

Simulation study report (PDF)

Simulation Python Causal Discovery

Hover to learn more

  • Extensive simulation sweep to guide hyperparameter selection for score‑based causal discovery (BIC / penalty discount).
  • Evaluated multiple graph configurations (ER vs scale‑free), sizes (20 vs 100 vars), degrees (2 vs 6), and sample sizes.
  • Practical takeaway: penalty discounts around ~0.6–1.0 often gave the best F1 (balancing precision/recall), especially with resampling.
